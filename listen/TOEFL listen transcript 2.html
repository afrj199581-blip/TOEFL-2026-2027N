التفريغ الكامل والمفصل للريكورد الثاني (Listening Test 2)

يحتوي هذا الملف على التفريغ الحرفي للريكورد الثاني كاملاً مع الأسئلة، الإجابات، والشرح المبسط.
(ملاحظة: تم تدارك النقص الموجود في ملف الوورد الأصلي لبعض الأسئلة في الأجزاء B و C ليكتمل الامتحان بـ 50 سؤالاً كما يجب).

Part A: Short Sentences

Question 1

Speaker: "Whatever movie you choose, I'll be happy to see it."
(D) I'll accept the movie you recommend.
التوضيح: أي فيلم تختاره سأكون سعيداً برؤيته. (أي سأقبل الفيلم الذي توصي به).

Question 2

Speaker: "Maria took their order, brought them coffee, and served the meal."
(A) Maria's probably a waitress.
التوضيح: أخذت طلبهم، أحضرت القهوة، وقدمت الوجبة. (مما يعني أنها على الأرجح نادلة).

Question 3

Speaker: "The copy machine's next to the encyclopedia table."
(C) The encyclopedia table's beside the copy machine.
التوضيح: آلة التصوير بجوار طاولة الموسوعات. (إذاً الطاولة بجوار آلة التصوير).

Question 4

Speaker: "The motorist has overtaken the train."
(B) The driver has passed the train.
التوضيح: سائق السيارة تجاوز (overtaken) القطار. (أي passed the train).

Question 5

Speaker: "Pat doesn't mind doing the laundry, nor does Jim."
(C) Both Pat and Jim will wash clothes without complaining.
التوضيح: بات لا تمانع غسل الملابس، وكذلك جيم. (كلاهما سيقوم بالعمل دون شكوى).

Question 6

Speaker: "She prefers not to work on group projects."
(A) She'd rather work alone.
التوضيح: هي تفضل ألا تعمل في مشاريع جماعية. (إذن هي تفضل العمل بمفردها).

Question 7

Speaker: "No doubt Bill's portfolio was the biggest."
(D) Bill's portfolio was certainly larger than the others.
التوضيح: لا شك أن محفظة أعمال بيل كانت الأكبر. (أي كانت بالتأكيد أكبر من البقية).

Question 8

Speaker: "Aren't the roses beautiful?"
(B) I think the roses are lovely.
التوضيح: أليست الورود جميلة؟ (هذا سؤال استنكاري يفيد الإعجاب، أي أعتقد أنها جميلة).

Question 9

Speaker: "Jane has a cousin in San Francisco, so she often goes there during the holidays."
(A) Jane usually visits San Francisco for her vacations.
التوضيح: جين تزور سان فرانسيسكو غالباً في العطلات لوجود ابن عمها هناك.

Question 10

Speaker: "The woman was disturbed by the noisy traffic."
(C) The woman was bothered by the loud cars and trucks.
التوضيح: المرأة انزعجت من حركة المرور الصاخبة. (bothered مرادف لـ disturbed).

Question 11

Speaker: "You went to that restaurant just last week?"
(B) Did you go eat there a week ago?
التوضيح: لقد ذهبت إلى ذلك المطعم الأسبوع الماضي فقط؟ (يتعجب أو يسأل عما إذا كان قد ذهب منذ أسبوع).

Question 12

Speaker: "Margaret looked up the definition in the unabridged dictionary."
(C) Margaret found the meaning of the word.
التوضيح: مارغريت بحثت عن التعريف في القاموس. (أي وجدت معنى الكلمة).

Question 13

Speaker: "She lacks the ability to complete the exercise."
(D) She can't finish her assignment.
التوضيح: هي تفتقر إلى القدرة على إكمال التدريب. (أي لا تستطيع إنهائه).

Question 14

Speaker: "Even as we speak, Mark's trying to compute the answers to his math problems on his calculator."
(A) Mark's working on his schoolwork now.
التوضيح: حتى ونحن نتحدث الآن، يحاول مارك حساب إجابات الرياضيات. (أي أنه يعمل على واجباته الآن).

Question 15

Speaker: "The exams will be corrected by noon."
(D) The tests will be graded by noon.
التوضيح: سيتم تصحيح الامتحانات بحلول الظهيرة. (graded مرادف لـ corrected).

Question 16

Speaker: "Mike was named to the student council."
(B) Mike became a member of the student council.
التوضيح: تم تعيين مايك في مجلس الطلاب. (أي أصبح عضواً فيه).

Question 17

Speaker: "You'd better hurry. Take five minutes too long and you'll miss the bus."
(C) You should hurry to catch the bus.
التوضيح: من الأفضل أن تسرع وإلا ستفوتك الحافلة. (يجب أن تسرع للحاق بها).

Question 18

Speaker: "Bill gives priority to extremely sick patients."
(C) Bill will treat very sick people first.
التوضيح: بيل يعطي الأولوية للمرضى الذين يعانون من حالة سيئة جداً. (أي يعالجهم أولاً).

Question 19

Speaker: "Even though spring's a few weeks away, it's quite warm today."
(D) It's quite a warm day for winter.
التوضيح: على الرغم من أن الربيع تفصلنا عنه أسابيع قليلة (مما يعني أننا في الشتاء)، إلا أن الجو دافئ اليوم.

Question 20

Speaker: "I couldn't believe that he was unprepared."
(B) I was surprised that he wasn't ready.
التوضيح: لم أصدق أنه كان غير مستعد. (أي كنت مندهشاً من عدم جاهزيته).
Part B: Short Conversations

Question 21

Woman: I found my briefcase.
Man: Just a moment. That's my briefcase. Yours is smaller, isn't it?
Question: What does the woman mean?
(C) Their briefcases are different sizes.
التوضيح: حقيبتها أصغر من حقيبته، مما يعني أن حقائبهما بأحجام مختلفة.

Question 22

Woman: The history assignment was difficult. I worked all night and couldn't finish it.
Man: You worked all night? It took me only 30 minutes.
Question: Why is the man surprised?
(D) He finds it hard to believe how much time the woman spent.
التوضيح: الرجل مندهش لأنها عملت طوال الليل بينما استغرق هو 30 دقيقة فقط.

Question 23

Man: Would you like me to get you some coffee?
Woman: Not now, thanks. Maybe later.
Question: What does the woman want?
(A) To drink some coffee in a while.
التوضيح: المرأة قالت 'ليس الآن، ربما لاحقاً'، مما يعني أنها تريد شرب القهوة بعد قليل.

Question 24

Man: Let's go for a walk at the beach today.
Woman: Why not? It's too nice to stay indoors.
Question: What does the woman mean?
(C) She'd rather be outside today.
التوضيح: المرأة تقول إن الجو أجمل من أن يبقوا في الداخل، لذا تفضل الخروج.

Question 25

Woman: Can I still get dinner in the cafeteria tonight?
Man: If you get in line quickly.
Question: What will probably happen soon?
(B) The cafeteria will close.
التوضيح: الرجل يخبرها بأن تسرع وتقف في الطابور، مما يدل على أن الكافتيريا ستغلق قريباً.

Question 26

Man: That's a nice cake. Did you make it yourself?
Woman: Not exactly. My roommate helped with the frosting.
Question: What does the woman mean?
(B) She made the cake with some assistance.
التوضيح: قالت إن زميلتها ساعدتها في التزيين، أي أنها صنعت الكعكة ببعض المساعدة.

Question 27

Man: Would you tell me what time the dormitory doors will be locked?
Woman: No problem. In fact, I can give you a copy of the dormitory rule book.
Question: What does the man want to know?
(A) The dormitory hours.
التوضيح: الرجل سأل عن موعد إغلاق أبواب السكن، وهو ما يعني السؤال عن ساعات وقواعد السكن.

Question 28

Woman: Was Roger upset when he heard the news?
Man: Oh, he really fell apart.
Question: How did Roger feel?
(B) He was devastated.
التوضيح: تعبير 'fell apart' يعني انهار عاطفياً أو دُمر (devastated).

Question 29

Man: I've run out of time. Can we finish this problem tomorrow?
Woman: Great. I'll see you then.
Question: What is the man's problem?
(D) He doesn't have enough time.
التوضيح: الرجل يقول 'لقد نفد وقتي'، أي ليس لديه وقت كافٍ (doesn't have enough time).

Question 30

Man: How much of a tip should we leave?
Woman: Not more than a dollar. The service was slow and the soup was cold.
Question: Where does this conversation probably take place?
(A) In a restaurant.
التوضيح: يتحدثان عن البقشيش (tip) والخدمة والشوربة، مما يؤكد أنهما في مطعم.

Question 31

Woman: Wasn't the anthropology lecture fascinating?
Man: Fascinating? It was too long and drawn out for me.
Question: How did the man feel about the lecture?
(D) He thought it was boring.
التوضيح: المرأة وجدتها رائعة، بينما وجدها الرجل طويلة ومطولة جداً (drawn out)، أي مملة.

Question 32

Woman: Are there enough drinks in the refrigerator?
Man: Enough. Any more and we'll need another refrigerator.
Question: What does the man mean?
(C) They have plenty of drinks.
التوضيح: الرجل يقول إن المشروبات كثيرة لدرجة أنهم إذا أحضروا المزيد سيحتاجون ثلاجة أخرى (لديهم وفرة).

Question 33

Man: Have you completed the paper for history class?
Woman: No. I can't seem to get things done in time.
Question: What does the woman mean?
(B) She rarely gets her work done when she should.
التوضيح: المرأة تقول إنها لا تستطيع إنجاز الأمور في وقتها، أي أنها نادراً ما تنهي عملها في الوقت المحدد.

Question 34

Man: The student council's meeting is about the parking issue.
Woman: I thought that was put off until next week.
Question: What does the woman mean?
(B) The decision was postponed.
التوضيح: المرأة تقول 'اعتقدت أنه تم تأجيله للأسبوع القادم'، (put off = postponed).
تمت إضافة هذا السؤال لإكمال التفريغ الناقص.

Question 35

Man: Are you going to the meeting?
Woman: I have to.
Question: What does the woman mean?
(C) She doesn't want to go.
التوضيح: المرأة ترد بـ 'مضطرة لذلك' (I have to)، مما يدل ضمناً على أنها لا ترغب في الذهاب لكنها مجبرة.
تمت إضافة هذا السؤال لإكمال التفريغ الناقص.
Part C: Short Talks
Talk 1 (Questions 36-40) Woman: Hey, Gloria. How would you like to increase the extent of your educational and historical background?
Gloria: Steve, I don't understand what you're saying at all. I just took my final in History 101 this morning, and I'm trying to get rid of the books.
Steve: They cost $80. Why don't you try to sell them back to the bookstore?
Gloria: I tried, but they'd only refund $20, and I paid so much more for them. I'd like to get at least $40.
Steve: Well, I'm not going to take History 101, so I'm not really interested in those books. Maybe you should ask some other friends.
Gloria: I already have. Everyone I know has already taken History 101 and doesn't want those books.
Steve: Why don't you put up some advertisements in the history building? Maybe someone you don't know will call you and buy them.
Gloria: I'll try it, but I don't think that'll work.
Steve: Then you'll have to go back to the bookstore. $20 is better than nothing.

Question 36

Question: Where does this conversation probably take place?
(C) On a university campus.
التوضيح: يتحدثان عن امتحانات نهاية الفصل ومبنى التاريخ والمكتبة، مما يؤكد أنهما في حرم جامعي.
الأسئلة 36-40 تمت إضافتها بناءً على نص المحادثة لتعويض النقص في التفريغ الأصلي.

Question 37

Question: What is the topic of the conversation?
(D) Ways to sell a used book.
التوضيح: الموضوع الأساسي هو محاولة إيجاد طريقة لبيع كتب التاريخ المستعملة.

Question 38

Question: Why does Gloria want to sell her books?
(C) She's finished using them.
التوضيح: غلوريا تقول إنها للتو أنهت امتحانها النهائي، لذا لم تعد بحاجة للكتب.

Question 39

Question: Why doesn't Gloria want to sell to the bookstore?
(B) She wouldn't get enough money.
التوضيح: المكتبة ستعيد لها 20$ فقط، وهي ترى أن هذا المبلغ غير كافٍ.

Question 40

Question: What does Steve suggest Gloria do?
(A) She should post some advertisements.
التوضيح: ستيف اقترح عليها وضع بعض الإعلانات (advertisements) في مبنى التاريخ.
Talk 2 (Questions 41-45) Speaker: The construction of the first transcontinental railroad was undertaken in 1862 by two competing railroad companies. The Union Pacific started in Omaha, Nebraska, and moved westward. The Central Pacific began in Sacramento, California, and moved eastward. Of the two, the Central Pacific had the more difficult task because it was faced with traversing the Sierra Nevadas. To lay tracks across these mountains, workers had to carve out footpaths on steep mountain faces and then use dynamite to blast out access for the railroad tracks. After years of dangerous and exhausting labor, the workers from the Central Pacific met up with the workers from the Union Pacific near Ogden, Utah, on May 10, 1869. In an exuberant ceremony, the last of the tracks was nailed to the ground with a golden spike. The completion of the railroad marked the beginning of a new era in transportation.

Question 41

Question: Who built the first transcontinental railroad?
(D) Two separate railroad companies.
التوضيح: تم بناء السكة الحديدية بواسطة شركتين متنافستين (Union Pacific و Central Pacific).

Question 42

Question: What was difficult about the job the Central Pacific workers had to complete?
(A) They had to lay tracks across a mountain range.
التوضيح: واجهوا صعوبة لأنهم اضطروا لعبور سلسلة جبال سييرا نيفادا ووضع مسارات عبرها.

Question 43

Question: How is the job of a railroad worker described?
(C) Dangerous.
التوضيح: وصف النص العمل بأنه خطير ومرهق (dangerous and exhausting labor).

Question 44

Question: How long did it take to complete the first transcontinental railroad?
(D) Several years.
التوضيح: بدأ العمل في 1862 وانتهى في 1869، مما يعني استغراق عدة سنوات (Several years).

Question 45

Question: What happened at the ceremony marking the completion of the railroad?
(B) A golden spike was used to complete the tracks.
التوضيح: في الحفل الختامي، تم تثبيت المسار الأخير بمسمار ذهبي (golden spike).
Talk 3 (Questions 46-50) Woman: I was reading in last night's paper that the utility company wants to build a solar energy plant in the desert not far from here. Do you think that's a good idea?
Man: A good idea? It's a great idea. Solar energy is the energy of the future. It's clean, it's safe, and it's abundant. What could be better?
Woman: Won't the utility company just raise our rates to pay for this new plant? The newspaper said that the utility company will need extra money to get the plant going.
Man: But in the long run, rates will be lower. And to have a constant supply of energy, that's a small price to pay.
Woman: I'm not convinced that the price will be so small.

Question 46

Question: How did the man learn about the new solar energy plant?
(B) From the newspaper.
التوضيح: الرجل علم بالأمر عندما أخبرته المرأة أنها قرأت عنه في جريدة الليلة الماضية (last night's paper).

Question 47

Question: Where will the solar energy plant be constructed?
(B) Close by.
التوضيح: المصنع سيبنى في الصحراء ليس بعيداً من هنا (not far from here)، أي بالقرب منهم (Close by).

Question 48

Question: According to the man, what are the benefits of solar energy?
(D) It exists in large quantities.
التوضيح: الرجل قال إنها نظيفة، آمنة، ووفيرة (abundant)، والوفرة تعني وجودها بكميات كبيرة.

Question 49

Question: How does the man feel about the cost of the plant?
(C) It'll be worth it in the long term.
التوضيح: الرجل يعتقد أن المعدلات ستنخفض على المدى الطويل (in the long run)، لذا الأمر يستحق.

Question 50

Question: How does the woman feel about the proposed solar energy plant?
(A) She's concerned it'll be too costly.
التوضيح: المرأة قلقة من أن الشركة سترفع الأسعار لدفع تكاليف المصنع، وقالت إنها غير مقتنعة بأن السعر سيكون قليلاً.
